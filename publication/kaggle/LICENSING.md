The repository's existing MIT `LICENSE` is retained for software. It does not override the separately scoped terms for data and documentation.

The dataset retains source URLs and locators. Full regulator decisions and PDF archives are not included. A summary or annotation is Legiscope's authored contribution; its existence does not make the underlying source a Legiscope work. Source labels preserve the recorded review depth and do not certify a complete legal audit.

When reusing the original contributions, credit Legiscope, link to the [study](https://www.legiscope.com/blog/amendes-rgpd-europe-comparaison-pays.html) and [this release](https://github.com/legiscope/gdpr-data-study/tree/v2026.10.30.1), link to CC BY 4.0, retain the relevant source and coverage notes, and identify your changes.
